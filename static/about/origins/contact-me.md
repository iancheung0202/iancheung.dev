There is this popular belief that most students don't check their emails or don't read them thoroughly, specially in middle/high school. There is always this pre-conceived notion that there needs to be an extra way to deliver the same message (through a different platform or in real life) in order to have it reach us properly. 

I find that rather humiliating. Ever since I have owned my first personal Gmail and later my first school email, **I have always taken my time to read** (and respond to if needed) any messages that come to my way every day. Even though sometimes I feel betrayed wasting my time reading irrelevant ones, I would still feel like I learned something new every time *(like there is a football game near me that I won't go, or a demonstration of how not to write an email)*.

As per normal email etiquette, [don't ask to ask](https://dontasktoask.com) or just [say hello](https://nohello.net/). Open your email with your thoughts and questions right away, and I would love to get to know you in turn. I try my best to respond thoughtfully to any well-intentioned emails, even if we have never met.
